# Header — Product context

## Purpose

Header is a personal planning workspace that brings daily and weekly organization, calendar, progress, finances, and focus into one calm interface. It should help one person understand the day quickly, plan the week without layout shifts, and act without having to decode a dashboard full of disconnected cards.

## Primary user and scene

The product is used by an individual throughout the day on desktop and mobile. The user returns frequently to check priorities, organize time, register progress, review money, and start focused work. The experience should feel dependable and mature rather than playful or gamified.

## Core capabilities

- Visão geral: current day, priorities, progress, and useful signals.
- Semana: stable seven-day planning with editable morning, afternoon, and evening periods.
- Calendário: date navigation and an agenda that makes scheduled work easy to scan.
- Evolução: progress and habit trends over time.
- Financeiro: balances, planned spending, transactions, and useful financial summaries.
- Pomodoro: a focused timer and recent focus history.
- Perfil e personalização: identity, theme, accent color, and the settings previously exposed as a separate Preferências section.
- Authentication and persistence of the user's real workspace data.

## Confirmed information architecture

The primary navigation has six sections: Visão geral, Semana, Calendário, Evolução, Financeiro, and Pomodoro. Preferências is not a primary destination. Its settings belong inside the profile/personalization experience.

## Visual commitments

- Clean, modern, mature, and deliberately quieter than the previous liquid-glass interface.
- A continuous work surface with fewer independent blocks.
- Use typography, whitespace, alignment, fine rules, charts, timelines, tables, and direct manipulation before introducing a card.
- Cards are reserved for objects that have their own action, state, or boundary.
- Dark appearance uses a matte near-black/graphite canvas.
- Light appearance uses a warm, comfortable off-white canvas.
- System appearance follows the operating-system preference.
- The existing customizable accent color remains functional in both light and dark appearances.
- Portuguese remains the product language.
- Data should look real and useful; avoid decorative placeholder content.

## Product principles

1. One glance before one click: the most useful status is visible without opening another panel.
2. Stable planning: editing one day must not make the remaining week jump or resize.
3. Interaction over decoration: motion explains selection, progress, or hierarchy and is never ambient noise.
4. One visual family: all six sections share the same density, type scale, spacing rhythm, and controls.
5. Contextual settings: personalization lives with the profile, not beside planning destinations.
6. Calm density: show enough information to be useful while preserving generous breathing room.

## Accessibility and behavior

- Preserve clear keyboard focus and sensible reading order.
- Maintain legible contrast in light, dark, and user-selected accent combinations.
- Keep touch targets comfortable on mobile.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Responsive layouts should reorganize structurally rather than simply shrink desktop columns.
